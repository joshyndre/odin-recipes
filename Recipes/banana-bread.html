Banana Bread

Description

A moist, sweet loaf made from ripe bananas — a great way to use up bananas that are past their prime. It's simple to make with one bowl and no mixer required, and it's just as good toasted the next day.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 4x8 inch loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until smooth.
  3. Stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas.
  4. Mix in the sugar, egg, and vanilla extract.
  5. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir in.
  6. Add the flour and mix until just combined; do not overmix.
  7. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for 55–65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Let cool in the pan for a few minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.
