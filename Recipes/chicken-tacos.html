Chicken Tacos

Description

Juicy, seasoned chicken folded into warm tortillas with fresh toppings. This is a quick weeknight meal that comes together in one pan and can be easily customized with your favorite salsas and garnishes.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. In a bowl, toss the chicken with olive oil, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the chicken for 5–7 minutes per side, until cooked through.
  3. Let the chicken rest for a few minutes, then slice or shred it.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  5. Fill each tortilla with chicken and top with onion and cilantro.
  6. Serve with lime wedges to squeeze over the top.
