Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

A classic Italian pasta dish made with eggs, cheese, cured pork, and black pepper. It comes together in about 20 minutes and relies on technique rather than a long ingredient list for its rich, creamy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 8 ounces pancetta or guanciale, diced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, fry the pancetta in a skillet over medium heat until crisp; add the garlic for the last minute.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, Pecorino Romano, Parmesan, and a generous amount of black pepper.
  4. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
  5. Add the hot spaghetti to the skillet with the pancetta, off the heat, and toss to combine.
  6. Quickly stir in the egg and cheese mixture, adding splashes of reserved pasta water until creamy.
  7. Serve immediately with extra cheese and black pepper on top.
